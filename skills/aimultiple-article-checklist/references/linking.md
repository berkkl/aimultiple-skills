# Linking — detail

## Anchor text

- Describe the destination accurately.
- Do not use misleading anchor text. Linking "DLP" to a Symantec product page is misleading.
- Avoid long anchor text on non-customer vendor links (do not drive traffic away for free).

## Internal links (to AIMultiple content)

- No "open in new tab". Internal links should flow.
- Verify every internal link points to `research.aimultiple.com` or `aimultiple.com`. WordPress has a bug where it rewrites internal links to `research-headless.aimultiple.com`. Check manually after saving.
- Internal linking is a major growth driver. Time on site grows, Google reads that as a positive signal.
- Every article must include a link to the most relevant AIMultiple vendor list (CTA). Example: an article on synthetic data links to `/synthetic-data-generator`.

## External links (outside AIMultiple)

- "Open in new tab" enabled. Users must be able to come back.
- Use `[efn_note]` format for references.
- Run every external source through `websiteseochecker.com/domain-authority-checker/`:
  - Spam risk >1 is a red flag.
  - Domain authority <40 is a red flag.
  - Exceptions: datasets, case studies, or the official source for a specific real-life example when no suitable alternative exists, AND the source is clearly trustworthy.
- Do not link to non-customer B2B vendors for free. Customers pay for placement; giving links away dilutes the value.

## Customer links

Customer URLs must carry the AIMultiple tracking tag so the customer can attribute incoming traffic to AIMultiple. See AIMultiple customer link guidelines for the exact tag format.

Prefer customer URLs that contain both:
- Social proof (testimonials, case studies, named logos).
- A form where the reader can leave contact information.

Example of a good customer URL: `https://www.haptik.ai/request-demo`.

## Call to action

Every article has at least one CTA:
1. A link to a relevant AIMultiple vendor list.
2. Links to relevant AIMultiple articles (internal linking).
3. Optional: a "Further reading" section with a few curated links.
