# Sponsorship handling — detail

AIMultiple sells article placement. Most articles will eventually have sponsors. The rules below protect both the reader experience and the sponsor relationship.

## TOFU vs BoFU

- TOFU articles (top of funnel, informational) should not include sponsored sections. Readers at this stage are not ready to compare vendors; sponsored content here feels like ads.
- BoFU articles (bottom of funnel, shortlist intent) concentrate sponsored content. Readers here expect vendor recommendations.

## Sponsored section requirements

Every sponsored section must include a link to a page where the reader can reach a conversion form. "Conversion form" means a page with a button or field for the reader to leave contact information so the vendor receives the lead.

- Good: `https://www.haptik.ai/request-demo` (has social proof + contact form).
- Bad: a generic vendor content page with no way for the reader to contact the vendor. If the reader has to open a new tab and search for the vendor, the lead is lost and AIMultiple gets no credit.

Up to two links per sponsored section is acceptable.

## Do not click sponsor links

Clicking sponsor links manually looks like click fraud to the sponsor. It inflates clicks without delivering readers and damages the relationship. One accidental click is survivable; a habit is not.

## Non-sponsor vendor mentions

Default: do not mention non-sponsors.

Exceptions:
1. Top-solutions lists where the leader of the category cannot be omitted without looking ignorant. Example: an "RPA software" best-of list has to name UiPath.
2. Articles about a direct competitor of a sponsor. Example: a process mining article about Celonis must name Celonis.
3. Industry-analyst reports where the value of the article is the named list.

When an exception applies:
- Use precise, sourced language. "Automation Anywhere, named as one of the top three RPA companies by Gartner" is acceptable. "Automation Anywhere, one of the top three RPA companies" is not.
- Minimize anchor text and link visibility for the non-sponsor.
- Prefer anchor text like "trade finance case study" over a vendor-branded anchor.
- Put the non-sponsor link in a "Sources" section when possible, not inline in a prominent sentence.

## Absolutist language

Never write "X is the best solution for Y". There are millions of companies and no solution is best for all of them. Customers read this and immediately challenge the claim.

Acceptable pattern:
> X is the only solution in our benchmark that covers all functionality manufacturing SMBs require in category Y. We recommend manufacturing SMBs shortlist X.

The acceptable version names the scope (our benchmark), the audience (manufacturing SMBs), and the reason (functionality coverage).

## Transparency

We are not a sales organization. Every sentence must be fact-based and truthful. Sponsors pay for placement; they do not pay for misleading claims on their behalf.
