# Surfer SEO workflow — detail

## Content Editor

Write the article inside Surfer Content Editor against the target keyword URL.

- Create the Content Editor by pasting the target slug (example: `whatsapp-business-api`) in the search bar.
- Click "Customize" at the top right and verify the competitors Surfer picked are high quality. If Surfer included low-quality competitors, the content score target is unreliable.
- Use the editor to manage titles, headings, bullets, bold, images, alt text, meta description, internal links.

## Score targets

- Minimum: content score >66.
- Must also exceed the average content score of the page-1 competitors.
- Ideal: higher than the highest-scoring page-1 competitor.

## Keyword coverage

- Read every suggestion from Surfer before accepting it. Many do not fit naturally.
- Use "Insert terms" selectively. Double-check each recommendation in context.
- Check heading coverage and keyword density. Aim to meet structure metrics without stuffing.

## Overflow strategy

If Surfer keeps demanding more keywords or more text to raise the score, and integrating them into the main body would degrade the reading experience, create an "FAQ by RankMath" section at the bottom of the article. Put the SEO-driven coverage there so the main article stays tight.

## Do not stuff

- Never accept keyword stuffing to hit the score target.
- A natural-reading article at score 66 beats a stuffed article at score 80 for both readers and long-run rankings.
- Surfer optimization should not produce what Cem calls "SEO-optimized garbage".
